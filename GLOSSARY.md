# Bingo Cards

A tool from Together, Not For for turning someone's goals for the year into a 5×5 bingo card they can print or share.

## Language

### Building a card

**Category**:
A named collection of ready-made items (Basic, Spicy, …) that auto-fill draws from.
_Avoid_: Theme, pack, set

**Style**:
A named set of card colors and sizes (Classic, …) applied to a card's look.
_Avoid_: Theme, preset, skin

**Appearance**:
Whether the app itself is shown light or dark; it never changes the card.
_Avoid_: Theme, mode, dark theme

### Sharing

**Shared Card**:
A saved card opened by its link; private to whoever has the link and never listed in search.
_Avoid_: Public card, published card

### Brand

**Brand Mark**:
The Together, Not For ring-and-heart symbol; the source of the product's icons and colors.
_Avoid_: Logo icon, favicon art
